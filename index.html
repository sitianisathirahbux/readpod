READ-POD & Whisper Phone Setup

  1. Whisper Phone: Hold the physical PVC Whisper Phone tube from mouth to ear.
  2. Listen First: Tap Listen to hear the teacher's native model pronunciation.
  3. Read Aloud: Tap Read Aloud and whisper the target word clearly into the phone microphone.
  4. Instant Feedback: The app will highlight the text Green when correctly pronounced!
